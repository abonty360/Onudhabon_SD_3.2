About Onudhabon

Exploring Education Through Community

Onudhabon is a non-profit educational initiative dedicated to bridging the educational divide across Bangladesh. We connect volunteer educators, local guardians, and underprivileged students to deliver high-quality digital learning resources completely free of cost.

Launch Full App

Our Mission

To democratize access to quality education by providing structured, curriculum-aligned video lectures and comprehensive study materials to every student regardless of geographic or financial boundaries.

Our Vision

A future where every child in Bangladesh has the mentorship, learning resources, and community encouragement needed to unlock their full academic potential and thrive.

What We Provide

Video Lectures

High quality, topic-wise video lessons for Classes 1 to 12.

Study Materials

Downloadable worksheets, notes, and exam practice sheets.

Community Forum

Open discussions between educators, guardians, and students.

Local Guardians

Grassroots mentors facilitating student learning & enrollment.

Platform Roles

System Admin

Approves volunteers, moderates content, manages rankings.

Educator

Uploads lectures and study materials; competes on monthly leaderboard.

Local Guardian

Enrolls students, tracks progress, evaluates lectures.

Donor

Contributes to the welfare pool via SSLCommerz secure payments.

Join Our Mission

Whether you want to contribute lectures as a volunteer educator or register underprivileged students as a local guardian, Onudhabon welcomes you.
